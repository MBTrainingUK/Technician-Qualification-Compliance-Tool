How these figures work

Mercedes-Benz Dealer Standards · Passenger Car & Van

Official qualification codes

PC MT MBPCQMT Van MT MBVQMT PC ST MBPCQST Van ST MBVQST PC DT MBPCQDT Van DT MBVQDT

How technicians are counted

  • Qualifications come from the official course codes above, on Passed status only.
  • Each technician counts in exactly one role: DT > ST > MT.
  • A DT beyond the scaled requirement is reallocated towards the 60% ST threshold.
  • If every technician is ST or DT, the MT requirement is waived.
  • Truck data is excluded.

Enrolled vs booked

  • Booked — an instructor-led or virtual classroom course with a date.
  • Enrolled — on the pathway but with no course date. A delegate can sit here indefinitely, which is why the two are counted separately.

The five requirements

  • 80% of technicians qualified at MT or above
  • 60% counted as Systems Technician
  • At least one MT-only technician (or waived)
  • At least one Diagnostic Technician
  • One DT per 15 technicians, scaled
